Stadium & Booking Guidelines

Entry & Gates

Stadium gates open 2 hours before match start. Carry a valid government photo ID alongside your digital or printed pass.

Prohibited Items

Outside food, beverages, sharp objects, lighters, backpacks, and helmets are not permitted inside the stadium bowl.

Ticket & Refund Policy

Tickets are verified at entry checkpoints and are non-transferable once scanned. Check official match advisories for weather updates.
