Stadium Guidelines

Ticket Verification

E-tickets or physical printed tickets must include a valid QR code. Screenshots of passes will not be accepted at entry gates.

ID Proof Requirement

A government-issued photo ID (Aadhaar Card, Driver's License, or Passport) must match the ticket holder's name for entry verification.

Prohibited Items

Outside food, beverages, laptops, helmets, glass bottles, power banks, and sharp objects are strictly prohibited inside the stadium premises.
